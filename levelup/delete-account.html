Delete your data

LevelUp · TBCore · Last updated 27 October 2026

Your training data is not with us. Workouts, records, photos and GPS routes live only on your phone. The only thing we can hold is the optional friends account (Google sign-in plus what you chose to share), and you can erase all of it yourself, in the app or by e-mail.

What account do we have?

LevelUp, published by TBCore, has no account of its own. If you use the optional friends feature, you sign in with Google and the app creates a small cloud profile for you in Firebase: your display name and avatar, a friend code, the stats snapshot you chose to share and your friendships. If you never signed in, there is nothing of yours with us to delete.

To delete your account and cloud data in the app

  1. Open LevelUp and go to Settings → Account & friends.
  2. Tap Delete account & cloud data.
  3. Type DELETE to confirm. Google may ask you to confirm your account once more.

That removes everything below straight away and signs you out.

If you no longer have the app

Write to support@tbcore.eu with the subject Delete my LevelUp account. Send it from the e-mail address of the Google account you signed in with, or include your friend code, so we can find the account. We delete it and confirm by reply, normally within a few days and at the latest within one month. You can also reinstall the app, sign in with the same Google account and delete it yourself as described above.

To switch off sharing without deleting your account

Turn off Share my stats with friends in the same settings block. Your shared snapshot is deleted from the cloud right away. Sign out stops everything on that phone.

To delete the data on your phone

Use Settings → Reset all data, or uninstall the app. Deleting your cloud account does not delete training data on the phone, and resetting the phone's data does not delete your cloud account, so do both if you want everything gone. Backup files you saved yourself, in Documents/LevelUp or elsewhere, are yours to delete.

What is deleted, and what is kept

  • Deleted: your friend profile (name, avatar, frame), your friend code, your shared stats and workouts, every friendship and request (so you also disappear from your friends' lists), and your sign-in record in Firebase Authentication, including your Google e-mail address and picture address stored there.
  • Kept on your phone: your training data, until you erase it yourself.
  • Not ours to delete: your Google account itself, and any technical logs Google keeps under its own policies, governed by the Google Privacy Policy. Google may keep residual copies in its backups for a limited period.

We keep no copy of a deleted account and no retention period of our own applies: once deleted, it is gone.
