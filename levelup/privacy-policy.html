Privacy Policy

LevelUp · TBCore · Last updated 27 October 2026

Your training stays on your phone. There is no advertising and no analytics. The only thing that can leave the device is what you choose to share with friends: that needs a Google sign-in and a switch you turn on yourself. You can use the whole app without ever signing in.

What we collect

Out of the box, nothing. LevelUp has no accounts of its own, no advertising and no analytics, and the app does not contact any server until you sign in. We do not know who you are, whether you train, or how you are doing, and we never sell or share anything about you.

That changes only if you turn on the optional friends feature. What leaves your phone then is described in Friends and sharing below.

What the app stores on your device

Everything you log is saved locally, in the app's own private storage (an on-device database). This includes:

  • Your profile: name, avatar, sex, age, height, body weight and goal, which LevelUp uses to compare your lifts with population strength standards
  • Workouts, sets, personal records, XP, ranks, achievements and streaks
  • Body weight and measurements, and progress photos if you add any
  • GPS routes, if you record a run, walk, cycle or hike
  • Your settings and your saved workouts and weekly plan

None of this is sent to us. It is removed when you uninstall the app, and you can erase it yourself at any time from Settings → Reset all data.

Friends and sharing

Settings → Account & friends is optional. It lets you compare progress with friends you add yourself. It works like this:

  • Sign-in. You sign in with a Google account. Google is used only to log you in. LevelUp receives a Firebase account identifier, your Google display name, e-mail address and profile picture address. The e-mail address and picture are kept in the sign-in record and are never shown to your friends.
  • Your friend profile. The app creates a profile with the name and avatar you use in LevelUp and a six-character friend code. Anyone who has your code can look up that name and avatar and send you a request. Nothing more is revealed until you accept. You can generate a new code at any time.
  • Sharing is off by default. Only if you switch on Share my stats with friends does the app upload a snapshot: level, XP, streak, sessions, total and weekly training volume, your strength rank, your estimated one-rep maximum on your main lifts, your recent personal records, and monthly boss progress. If you also switch on Also share my latest workouts, the exercises, sets, reps and weights of your last ten sessions are included.
  • Who can see it. Only friends you have accepted. Your friends see their own copy of your snapshot; nobody else can read it.
  • Never shared: progress photos, body weight, measurements, notes, GPS routes, your age, sex or height, and your e-mail address.
  • Where it is stored. In Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore), in a Firebase project operated by TBCore. The Firestore database is located in the European Union (multi-region eur3). Firebase Authentication is operated by Google and may process sign-in data outside the EU; see Where your data is processed.

Turning sharing off deletes your snapshot from the cloud. Deleting your account removes everything, see Deleting your data. TBCore has technical access to the Firebase project and only looks at it to keep the service running, to investigate abuse or to carry out a deletion request.

Google and Firebase

Signing in and sharing use Google's services. Google receives the technical data any online service receives, such as your IP address, device and app information, to deliver and secure them. That processing is governed by the Google Privacy Policy and the Firebase privacy information. We do not use Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics, advertising or any other measurement product. If you never sign in, the Firebase code is never started and nothing is sent.

Location

If you record a cardio session, LevelUp asks for location permission, and Android shows a notification while it records so GPS keeps running with the screen off. Your route, distance and pace are calculated and stored on your phone only. Location is used only while you are recording, is never uploaded and is never part of what you share with friends.

Garmin watch

If you install the optional LevelUp app on a Garmin watch, your phone and watch exchange your workout (exercises, sets, reps, weights, heart rate and the result) through the Garmin Connect app on your phone, using Garmin's Connect IQ technology. LevelUp does not send this to us. What Garmin does with data in Garmin Connect is governed by Garmin's privacy policy.

Backups

  • Manual backup. Settings → Backup & restore saves your data as a file in the Documents/LevelUp folder of your phone, and can hand it to the share sheet. Where you send it is up to you; we never see it. LevelUp also makes a weekly safety copy in the same folder.
  • Android Backup. If device backup is on in your Android settings, the operating system may include the app's data in your personal Google backup, so a new phone can restore it. That is handled by Android and Google, not by us. You can turn it off in your device's backup settings.

Voice coach and notifications

The optional voice coach plays audio clips that are included in the app (generated with an AI speech model), and may use your phone's own text-to-speech engine. It does not record you. It has an Explicit language switch: one persona uses strong profanity when that is on. Reminders and rest-timer alerts are notifications scheduled on your own phone; nothing about them is sent anywhere.

Advertising

There is none, and there will not be. LevelUp contains no adverts, no advertising SDKs and no advertising identifiers. There are no in-app purchases.

Leaderboard

The “Simulated players” leaderboard is generated on your device. Those names are not real people and nothing is exchanged. Real friends appear only through the friends feature above.

Who is responsible

TBCore (Denmark) is the controller of the personal data processed through the optional friends feature. You can reach us at support@tbcore.eu. For everything that stays on your phone, we do not receive or process it at all.

Legal basis (GDPR)

  • Creating your friend profile, signing you in and sharing your snapshot with the friends you accept: performing the service you asked for and your consent (you opt in by signing in and again by switching sharing on; you can withdraw at any time by switching it off or deleting your account).
  • Keeping the service secure and preventing abuse: our legitimate interest.

Where your data is processed

Firestore data is stored in the EU. Google operates Firebase worldwide, so sign-in and technical data may be processed by Google in other countries, including the United States, under the safeguards Google describes in its terms, such as the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and standard contractual clauses.

How long we keep it

Cloud data is kept until you delete it: switching sharing off removes your snapshot, and deleting your account removes your profile, friend code, snapshot, friendships and sign-in record. Google may keep residual copies in its backups for a limited period under its own policies. Data on your phone stays until you erase it or uninstall.

Deleting your data

Inside the app: Settings → Account & friends → Delete account & cloud data. Or write to us if you no longer have the app. Step by step on the delete-account page. Deleting your cloud account does not touch your training data on the phone.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you may ask us for access to your data, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, and you may object to processing or withdraw consent. Write to support@tbcore.eu; we reply within one month. You also have the right to complain to the Danish Data Protection Agency (Datatilsynet, datatilsynet.dk) or to your local supervisory authority.

Children

LevelUp is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly create friend profiles for them. If you believe a child has signed in, write to us and we will delete the account. It is not part of Google Play's Designed for Families programme.

Permissions

The app asks for these Android permissions, each for one purpose:

  • INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE — used only by the optional sign-in and friends feature
  • ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION, ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION, FOREGROUND_SERVICE, FOREGROUND_SERVICE_LOCATION — recording a cardio route; the app never asks for background location
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS, USE_EXACT_ALARM — rest-timer and training reminders, on time
  • VIBRATE, WAKE_LOCK — haptics and keeping the screen on during a workout
  • RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED — restoring scheduled reminders and the home-screen widget after a restart
  • READ/WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE (Android 10 and older only) — saving your backup file in Documents

The app does not request camera, microphone, contacts or SMS permissions.

Changes to this policy
