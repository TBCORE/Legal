Privacy Policy

Scrap to Stars · TBCore · Last updated 7 September 2026

We run no servers and receive nothing about you. There is no advertising and no analytics of ours. If you choose to turn on cloud save, your progress is stored in your own Google account through Google Play Games — not with us. You can play the whole game without ever signing in.

What we collect

Nothing reaches us. We have no servers, no accounts of our own and no analytics. We do not know who you are, whether you play, or how far you get, and we never sell or share anything about you, because we never have it.

That is not the same as saying nothing ever leaves your phone. If you turn on cloud save, it does — see below.

What the app stores on your device

Your progress is saved locally on your phone, in the app's own private storage. This includes:

  • The display name you choose when you first open the game
  • Your game progress — worlds, machines, currencies and upgrades
  • Your settings, such as whether sound is on

This is removed when you uninstall the app, and you can erase it yourself at any time from Settings → Erase all progress. It stays on the device unless you turn on cloud save.

Cloud save

Settings → Cloud save is optional and off until you sign in. It uses Google Play Games Services, and turning it on means:

  • You sign in with a Google account. Google Play Games creates or reuses a gamer profile for you, with a name and an avatar. That profile belongs to Google and to you, not to us, and the game does not read it — it is used only to know which account the save belongs to.
  • Your save — the same progress described above, including your display name — is copied to a private folder in your own Google Drive, reserved for this app. It travels over an encrypted connection.
  • The game reads that copy back when you reinstall or move to a new phone, and asks you which save to keep when the two differ.
  • Google's own SDK also collects diagnostics and analytics about itself. That is Google's collection, governed by the Google Privacy Policy, and we see none of it.

We cannot read your cloud save. It sits in your Google account, and you can delete it from your Play Games profile or from your Google account at any time — step by step here. If you would rather not sign in at all, the manual backup code below does the same job and involves nobody but you.

Backups

Two things can copy your save off the device, and both are entirely under your control:

  • Android Backup. If you have device backup enabled in your Android settings, the operating system may include this app's data in your personal Google Drive backup, so a new phone can restore your progress. This is handled by Android and Google, not by us — we have no access to it. You can turn it off in your device's backup settings.
  • Manual backup. Settings → Backup & restore shows your save as a piece of text you can copy. Where you put it is up to you. We never see it.

Advertising

There is none, and there will not be. Scrap to Stars contains no adverts, no advertising SDKs, and no advertising identifiers.

Purchases

The game offers optional one-time purchases and a monthly subscription. Every transaction is handled entirely by Google Play. We never see, receive or store your payment details, your billing address, or your Google account information. Google tells the app only whether a purchase completed, and the app stores that single fact on your device so your purchase keeps working.

Google's handling of the payment is governed by the Google Privacy Policy. Subscriptions are managed from your Google Play account, where you can also cancel them; we cannot see or change them on your behalf.

Purchases are tied to your Google account, not to us. If you reinstall the game or move to a new phone, Google restores them — nothing needs to be sent to us for that to work.

Leaderboards

The leagues in the game are generated on your device. The other names you see are simulated opponents, not real people. No information is exchanged with anyone, and your name is never sent anywhere.

Children

Scrap to Stars is suitable for a general audience and rated PEGI 3. Because we receive no data about anyone, we receive nothing about children either. It is not directed specifically at children under 13 and is not part of Google Play's Designed for Families programme.

Permissions

The app requests four permissions:

  • com.android.vending.BILLING — lets Google Play process a purchase
  • INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE — used by Google Play to reach its own servers and check whether you are online
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS — lets the app tell you when your offline tank is full and your machines have stopped earning. It is the only notification the app sends, it is scheduled on your own device, and you can refuse the permission or turn it off later without losing anything else

The first three belong to Google Play's billing library rather than to the game. The game has no networking code of its own, contacts no server of ours, and would work identically with the device in flight mode apart from the store. The notification is composed and scheduled entirely on your phone; nothing about it is sent anywhere. The app asks for nothing else — no storage, no location, no contacts, no camera. You can check the full list on the app's Play Store page under App permissions.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app changes what it does with data, this page will be updated and the date at the top will change. Material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes on Google Play.
