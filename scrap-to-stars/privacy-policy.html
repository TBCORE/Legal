Privacy Policy

Scrap to Stars · TBCore · Last updated 6 September 2026

Scrap to Stars does not collect any data. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no servers. The app does not request the Android internet permission, so it is not technically capable of transmitting anything.

What we collect

Nothing. We do not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share any personal information or usage data. We do not know who you are, whether you play, or how far you get.

What the app stores on your device

Your progress is saved locally on your phone, in the app's own private storage. This includes:

  • The display name you choose when you first open the game
  • Your game progress — worlds, machines, currencies and upgrades
  • Your settings, such as whether sound is on

None of this leaves your device. It is removed when you uninstall the app. You can erase it yourself at any time from Settings → Erase all progress.

Backups

Two things can copy your save off the device, and both are entirely under your control:

  • Android Backup. If you have device backup enabled in your Android settings, the operating system may include this app's data in your personal Google Drive backup, so a new phone can restore your progress. This is handled by Android and Google, not by us — we have no access to it. You can turn it off in your device's backup settings.
  • Manual backup. Settings → Backup & restore shows your save as a piece of text you can copy. Where you put it is up to you. We never see it.

Advertising

There is none, and there will not be. Scrap to Stars contains no adverts, no advertising SDKs, and no advertising identifiers.

Purchases

The in-app store is not active in this version. When paid items are introduced, purchases will be handled entirely by Google Play. We never see or store your payment details — Google tells the app only whether a purchase was completed. Google's own privacy policy governs that transaction.

Leaderboards

The leagues in the game are generated on your device. The other names you see are simulated opponents, not real people. No information is exchanged with anyone, and your name is never sent anywhere.

Children

Scrap to Stars is suitable for a general audience and rated PEGI 3. Because the app collects no data whatsoever, it collects nothing from children either. It is not directed specifically at children under 13 and is not part of Google Play's Designed for Families programme.

Permissions

The app requests no Android permissions at all. Not internet access, not storage, not location, not contacts, not the camera. You can verify this on the app's Play Store page under App permissions.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app changes what it does with data — for example when in-app purchases go live — this page will be updated and the date at the top will change. Material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes on Google Play.

Contact

Questions about this policy or the app can go to support@tbcore.dk.
