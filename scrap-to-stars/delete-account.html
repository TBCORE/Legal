Delete your data

Scrap to Stars · TBCore · Last updated 7 September 2026

We hold nothing to delete. Scrap to Stars has no accounts of ours and no servers of ours. Your progress is either on your phone or in your own Google account, and both are yours to erase without asking us.

There is no account with us

Scrap to Stars, published by TBCore, does not create an account for you and does not keep a copy of anything you do. The only sign-in in the game is an optional Google sign-in, used by Google Play Games Services so that cloud save can put your progress in your own Google account. We cannot read it, and we have no database in which you appear.

To delete your progress on the phone

  1. Open the game.
  2. Go to Settings → Erase all progress.

Uninstalling the app removes the same data.

To delete your cloud save

The cloud save lives in a private folder in your own Google Drive, reserved for this app. Remove it from your Google account:

  1. Open the Google Play Games app.
  2. Go to More → Settings → Delete Play Games account and data.
  3. Choose to delete the data for Scrap to Stars only, or the whole Play Games account.

You can also manage the app's hidden Drive data at drive.google.com/drive/settings under Manage apps.

What is deleted, and what is kept

  • Deleted: your display name, your game progress — worlds, machines, currencies and upgrades — and your settings. Both the copy on the phone and the copy in your Google account, once you do both steps above.
  • Kept, because it is not ours to delete: your record of purchases, which belongs to your Google Play account. Google keeps purchase history for its own accounting and legal obligations. It is governed by the Google Privacy Policy, and it is what lets you restore a purchase later on a new phone.

We keep nothing, so there is no retention period on our side. Nothing is held back, archived, or kept for a number of days before erasure — there is nothing in our hands at any point.

If you would rather ask a person

Write to support@tbcore.eu with the subject Delete my data. We will tell you exactly what to do and confirm that we hold no copy. We cannot reach into your Google account on your behalf, so the steps above will always be faster.
